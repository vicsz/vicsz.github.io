  <h1>Victor Szoltysek</h1>

  <p>
    I’m a principal-level technical leader working at the intersection of
    AI-enabled delivery, cloud architecture, DevOps, and developer productivity.
    I help teams turn ambitious modernization and AI ideas into practical
    workflows, stronger engineering capability, and measurable outcomes.
  </p>

  <p>
    I’m an AWS Gold Jacket, with all 14 active AWS certifications, and I stay
    close to the work: architecture, delivery, workshops, prototypes, and the
    operating habits that help teams move faster without creating more process
    for its own sake.
  </p>

  <p>
    I’m optimistic about AI, but skeptical of AI theatre. Useful adoption needs
    a real workflow, the right context and controls, and people who can evaluate
    the result—not just more generated output.
  </p>
